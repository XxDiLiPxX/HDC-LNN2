--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OBJECTIVE & BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This phase investigated score distribution separability and decision thresholding
strategies for continuous-time HDC-LNN anomaly detection across UNSW-NB15 (30,000 flows)
and NSL-KDD (22,544 flows).

Initial baseline configurations evaluated Mahalanobis distance thresholds using a
heuristic formula:
    threshold = mean(D_normal) + k * std(D_normal) (k=3.0)

While precision under this heuristic was exceptionally high (97.9% on UNSW, 92.5% on KDD),
it caused severe under-detection of subtle or stealthy attacks (recall: ~51.7% on UNSW,
~53.3% on KDD), resulting in an overall F1-score capped at ~0.677.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. METHODOLOGY & CALIBRATION MECHANISM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To resolve this without modifying model weights or violating leakage isolation:
1. Leakage Isolation: The ReferenceManifold continues to fit exclusively on normal
   validation states (label=0) to establish the true normal baseline distribution.
2. Validation Threshold Calibration: Using validation trajectories (both normal and
   attacks), candidate decision thresholds are evaluated across 1,000 empirical quantile
   steps to determine the operating point that maximizes the validation F1-score:
       threshold* = argmax_{th} F1(y_val, D_val > th)
3. Test Set Application: The calibrated threshold* is frozen and applied unconditionally
   to the entity-disjoint test split.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EMPIRICAL RESULTS & GAINS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### UNSW-NB15 Benchmark (30,000 flows, N_test = 4,613 flows):
- Baseline (k=3.0*std): F1 = 0.6771 | Precision = 0.9792 | Recall = 0.5174 | FN = 1,716
- Calibrated (F1-Max):  F1 = 0.8719 | Precision = 0.8963 | Recall = 0.8487 | FN = 538
- Performance Delta:    +0.1948 F1 (+28.8%) | -1,178 Missed Attacks (-68.6%)

### NSL-KDD Benchmark (22,544 flows, N_test = 3,466 flows):
- Baseline (k=3.0*std): F1 = 0.6761 | Precision = 0.9249 | Recall = 0.5327 | FN = 800
- Calibrated (F1-Max):  F1 = 0.8089 | Precision = 0.8183 | Recall = 0.7996 | FN = 343
- Performance Delta:    +0.1328 F1 (+19.6%) | -457 Missed Attacks (-57.1%)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SEPARABILITY CEILING FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Analysis of the latent state space (hidden_dim=64) indicates an intrinsic overlap
of ~44-47% between benign flow manifolds and stealthy attacks (e.g. low-and-slow DNS,
reconnaissance), explaining why FPR@95%TPR remains ~82% on UNSW and ~94% on KDD regardless
of covariance estimation technique.

Future work directions:
1. Increase liquid hidden state capacity (d=128/256)
2. Multi-scale continuous HDC encoding (RFF/SAX)
3. Extended sequence context horizons (>16 steps)
