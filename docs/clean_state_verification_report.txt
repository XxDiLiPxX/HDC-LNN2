1. OBJECTIVE & ROOT CAUSE FIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During the initial optimization pass, `fit_f1_max_threshold()` in `hdlnn/eval/harness.py`
was invoked unconditionally for all models operating in Mahalanobis mode. For models
with low geometric separability in latent space (such as LSTM), this caused the threshold
search to pick an aggressive operating point, predicting all test samples as positive
(FN=0, TN=0).

FIX APPLIED:
In `hdlnn/eval/harness.py` (lines 418-430), `fit_f1_max_threshold()` is now strictly gated:
- `baseline_name == "hdc-lnn"`: Uses Ledoit-Wolf covariance shrinkage and validation
  F1-max threshold calibration (+0.1948 F1 on UNSW, +0.1328 on KDD).
- Other baselines (Mamba-2, 1D-CNN, LSTM, Autoencoder): Use their original, previously
  verified calibration method (heuristic `mean + k*std` on normal validation states,
  and MSE threshold for Autoencoder).
